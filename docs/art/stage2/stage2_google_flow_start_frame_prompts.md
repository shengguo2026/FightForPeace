**Version:** 1.0 production draft  
**Purpose:** Generate text-free 16:9 start frames in Codex, then animate them as silent clips in Google Flow.

## 1. Use and continuity rules

For each shot, provide the relevant approved character and location references to the image generator. Replace bracketed asset tokens only after those references exist. Keep a seed/variant log and reuse the same accepted character sheets, village map, greenhouse layout, bikes, insignia, and palette in every generation.

Append this shared direction to every still-image prompt:

> Cinematic 2D cel-shaded action-game illustration, consistent with the supplied Fight for Peace reference images, clean confident linework, restrained painterly background detail, physically believable anatomy and architecture, readable foreground/midground/background separation, 16:9 composition designed for 1280×720 video, one clear camera axis, natural depth and parallax layers, no embedded words, no signage text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark. Preserve exact approved faces, ages, clothing, body proportions, equipment, color palettes, village geography, greenhouse construction, and time-of-day continuity from the supplied model sheets.

Shared negative constraints:

> No photorealism, no 3D-render appearance, no anime exaggeration, no childlike proportions, no sexualization, no military occupation imagery, no futuristic weapons, no magical wind or fire powers, no extra fingers or limbs, no duplicated people, no warped greenhouse structure, no impossible plants growing directly from animal tanks, no visibly dirty food water, no illegible pseudo-text, no baked captions, no camera-facing pose unless requested.

Flow motion prompts should request silent footage. Avoid visible speech where dubbing would require lip synchronization. Favor environmental movement, profile views, hands, reaction shots, and narrator-led action.

## 2. Chapter A — Prosperity Interlude

### P01 — Water returns

**Start-frame prompt:** A wide sunrise view downstream from the repaired Stage 1 river gate, clear water moving into restored irrigation channels, villagers standing at a safe overlook with baskets and tools, the elder in three-quarter profile in the foreground, previously dry fields beginning to green, hopeful but not luxurious, camera low enough to make the flowing water the visual path into the village.

**Flow motion:** Slow forward crane following the water; ripples and grasses move; villagers exchange relieved gestures; end with the channel leading toward distant fields. No speaking.

**Transition:** Match the water direction into P02.

### P02 — Building a reserve

**Start-frame prompt:** The same village several months later, coordinated workers shaping a lined water-reserve basin and installing modest gravity-fed channels, engineers and farmers consulting a diagram board whose surface is turned away from camera, hand tools and simple machinery used safely, elder observing beside local builders, midday construction dust and reflected water.

**Flow motion:** Sideways tracking shot; workers pass materials in a chain; a sluice opens and the first controlled stream enters the reservoir.

**Transition:** End on the stream entering a pipe.

### P03 — Seasons of construction

**Transition:** Push toward one completed greenhouse doorway.

## 8. Assembly and review checklist

- Lock character sheets before batch generation; reject identity drift immediately.
- Maintain a continuity sheet for clothing state, injuries, props, light direction, weather, and geography.
- Generate clean frames without text; add all localized words in the game or edit timeline.
- Confirm hydroponic/aquaponic plausibility with separated tanks, filtration, circulation, aeration, and accessible service paths.
- Preserve one canonical story result; use inserts only for performance variation.
- Use motion-safe camera paths and retain reduced-motion still sequences.
- Record source prompt, reference images, tool/model, date, variant, edits, rights evidence, and final asset hash.
- Review every generated person for anatomy, age, consented likeness use, costume anchors, and non-duplication.
- Review every clip transition before spending credits on extensions; extend only accepted identity-stable clips.

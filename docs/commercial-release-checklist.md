# Fight for Peace — Commercial Release Checklist

**Status:** Living checklist, started 2026-10-21. Nothing here is needed during development. Work through it once the creator is happy with the game's quality and decides to sell it.
**Not legal or tax advice.** Items marked ⚖️ should be confirmed with a lawyer or tax advisor before release.

## 1. Paid plans and service terms

| # | Item | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Flow Music (Google): paid plan, or clear written commercial terms** | Research on 2026-10-21 found no explicit commercial game licence for Flow Music output on the free plan. Until that is confirmed, every Flow Music track is flagged *development only* in the asset manifest. If no clear licence is available, replace the tracks with licensed or commissioned music (§2.2). | Open |
| 1.2 | **Google Flow (Veo video): confirm terms for the plan in use** | Research on 2026-10-21 found paid-game use reasonably well supported, with conditions. Re-check the terms at release time, and consider a paid plan if the free daily limit slows production. | Open |
| 1.3 | **Codex / OpenAI image generation: confirm the output terms** | All Codex pictures are generated by the creator's account; confirm commercial use rights for the plan in use at release time. | Open |
| 1.4 | **Cloudflare paid plan (Workers Paid)** | Only needed for online mode (room server, signalling, entitlement checks). The free plan is fine for demos; a paid plan gives higher request limits and Durable Objects for production load. | Open (after the slice) |
| 1.5 | **ElevenLabs paid plan, if it is used for SFX** | ElevenLabs free-tier output generally may not be used commercially. Only relevant if open-source SFX are later replaced with ElevenLabs sounds. | Not used yet |
| 1.6 | **GitHub plan** | Check that the private repositories (`FightForPeace`, `Pictures`) and Actions minutes fit the plan in use; a paid plan may be needed for CI volume. | Open |

## 2. Content rights and provenance

| # | Item | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Asset provenance audit** | Spec Appendix C.2: every final asset needs its source prompt, tool and model, references, approval date and replacement history. Verify that the manifest is complete and that no *development only* asset ships. | Open |
| 2.2 | **Final music** | Replace or license all Flow Music tracks (see 1.1); options are a royalty-free library licence or a commissioned composer (about 5–8 tracks for the slice). Keep the licence documents. | Open |
| 2.3 | **SFX licences and credits** | Open-source SFX: keep CC0 where possible; list every CC-BY sound with its required attribution in the credits screen; avoid non-commercial (NC) licences entirely. | Open |
| 2.4 | **Fonts** | The Chinese and Latin UI fonts must allow embedding in a commercial game (for example Noto Sans SC under the SIL OFL). Keep the licence files. | Open |
| 2.5 | **Third-party code licences** | Audit all npm dependencies (Phaser, Preact and others) for licence compatibility and add the required notices to the credits/legal screen. | Open |
| 2.6 | ⚖️ **Elder likeness consent** | The elder is based on the creator's own photo. Keep a short written record of the creator's consent and that the likeness may be used commercially and in marketing. | Open |
| 2.7 | ⚖️ **Name and trademark check** | Search whether *Fight for Peace* (and the Chinese title) is already a registered trademark for games in the target markets; consider registering it. Also secure a domain and store page name. | Open |

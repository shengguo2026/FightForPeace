# Fight for Peace — Campaign Music Direction

## Status and authority

This document defines the approved musical families and cue suites for campaign Stages 1–8 and the two initial Random Operations objective families. It extends the confirmed Authored Adaptive Score architecture in `Features_Decisions.md` and preserves the existing Stage 2 score without renaming or replacing its cues.

Before this decision, only Stage 2 had a complete stage-specific cue suite. Stage 1 defined the shared adaptive-score system but not its final musical family; Stages 3–8 and Random Operations had no cue suites.

All displayed cue names require stable localization keys and English, Simplified Chinese, and German presentation before shipping. The English names below are the approved source titles.

## Campaign-wide musical language

- A recurring five-note **village motif** represents protection, responsibility, and peace maintained through action. Every stage may transform its rhythm, orchestration, register, or harmony without losing recognition.
- A restrained three-note **sponsor motif** appears only as a subtle color in Stages 1–6, becomes intelligible during Stage 7, and is confronted directly in Stage 8. It may not disclose the Meridian Compact before the approved reveal.
- The **Wayfarer motif** begins as a searching road figure in Stage 4, matures through later Chronicle material, and joins the village motif voluntarily in Stages 7–8.
- Stage 5 uses an acoustic historical palette during playable past events. Modern framing may reintroduce present-day ambience and restrained electronic texture only after the return to the present.
- Music is presentation-only. It never controls timing, enemy decisions, random outcomes, score, or deterministic simulation.

## Stage 1 — The Broken Water Pact

**Musical family:** **Water Held Back**

**Palette:** Organic strings, airy flute, wooden percussion, restrained drums, mill rhythm, and processed water texture. The sound begins dry and rhythmically constrained, then opens after the river is restored.

1. **River Before Silence** — introduction and remembered normality.
2. **Fields Without Water** — dry-field arrival and investigation.
3. **Bridge Under Watch** — occupied-bridge pressure and ordinary combat.
4. **Mill in Motion** — machinery, workers, and environmental combat.
5. **Through the Dry Canal** — escalating canal route and approach to the gate.
6. **Keeper of the Gate** — Sluice Commander boss family.
7. **River Returned** — restored water, ending, results transition, and Museum playback.

Adding this suite does not modify Stage 1's route, encounters, story, difficulty identity, or locked gameplay content.

## Stage 4 — The Weight of Inheritance

**Musical family:** **Burden of Blood**

**Palette:** Traveling strings, wheel-like percussion, restrained modern rhythm, unresolved harmony, and the emerging Wayfarer motif.

1. **Records in Transit** — convoy departure and evidence responsibility.
2. **Doubt at the Waystation** — suspicion and civilian tension.
3. **Wheels under Pressure** — road pursuit and convoy defense.
4. **Three Who Stand Together** — Kael, Soren, and Chen Yue as a protective but misdirected group.
5. **Kael — Inherited Weight** — pressure-boxing and chain-wrestling boss family.
6. **Truth under Seal** — authenticated evidence review.
7. **A Road Chosen Freely** — Kael's neutrality and transition to the historical reconstruction.

**Adaptive and sound-priority rule:** *Burden of Blood* uses traveling strings, wheel-like percussion, restrained modern rhythm, unresolved harmony, and an emerging Wayfarer motif. The Wayfarer figure becomes progressively clearer through *Three Who Stand Together*, Kael's boss family, and *A Road Chosen Freely*; the sponsor motif appears only as a faint color during evidence examination and cannot reveal the Compact. Musical intensity follows deterministic encounter, pursuit, and boss-phase states but never controls gameplay. Evidence-seal and chain-of-custody confirmations, civilian-danger alerts, motorbike Stability warnings, Exact Block/Counter/Combo Breaker feedback, partner-rescue alerts, and Kael's readable grappling and phase cues take mix priority and trigger bounded music ducking. Every essential audio cue has a visual equivalent and optional controller-vibration equivalent. Music, ambience, dialogue, and combat effects have independent controls. Generated music is candidate source material only and requires human editing into clean adaptive loops, provenance records, and commercial-use verification.

## Curated Mission — Storm over Qinglan

**Musical family:** **Hands through the Rain**

**Palette:** Restrained strings, wooden percussion, low drums, rain-like shakers, warm winds, and a small hopeful community motif. The family emphasizes coordinated rescue rather than heroic conquest and remains distinct from Stage 3's *Mist and Resolve* mountain palette.

1. **Rain over Qinglan** — opening, distress response, and briefing.
2. **Road beneath the Runoff** — traversal and ordinary relief-looter combat.
3. **Guide Them Home** — rescue-condition pressure and short evacuations.
4. **The Storm Jackal** — Mara Venn's three boss phases.
5. **Shelter Lights** — coda, results transition, and Museum playback.

Segment-authored rain and runoff ambience remains separate from music. Decorative thunder may not imitate objective, debris, defensive, or boss warnings. Essential cues receive mixer priority, captions, and non-audio equivalents. The family reveals no sponsor motif or mandatory campaign information.

## Curated Mission — No Escape at Reedwater

**Musical family:** **River Without Tolls**

**Palette:** Plucked strings, woodblocks, restrained hand drums, low strings, river ambience, and a small ferry-bell motif. The family expresses public passage, coordinated apprehension, and working-waterfront tension rather than conquest. It contains no sponsor motif.

1. **Reedwater Before the Bell** — opening, ordinary ferry life, Council authorization, and briefing.
2. **Close the Crossing** — Ferry Market and Warehouse pursuit.
3. **Lines Under Tension** — Crane Yard, winch, custody, and escape-network pressure.
4. **The Tollmaster's Last Run** — Cassian Rook's mounted and ferry-deck phases.
5. **Passage Restored** — coda, results transition, and Museum playback.

Motorbike, relay, winch, crane cargo, barriers, restraint, ferry horn, ropes, water, and custody effects remain separate from music. Bells and horns cannot imitate objective or defensive warnings. Essential cues receive mixer priority, captions, and non-audio equivalents. Generated audio remains candidate material until edited, provenance-recorded, and verified for commercial use.

## Random Operations — Defeat

**Musical family:** **Five Waves, One Stand**

This family is rhythmically driven and designed for repeated combat without borrowing campaign-boss music.

1. **Operation Assembled** — hero and arena confirmation.
2. **First Contact** — Wave 1 and early Wave 2.
3. **Pressure Rising** — later Wave 2 and Wave 3.
4. **Fourth-Wave Breakpoint** — Wave 4 escalation.
5. **Final Wave** — Wave 5 and elite pressure.
6. **Field Secured** — victory and results.
7. **Regroup and Retry** — defeat or voluntary restart stinger.

## Random Operations — Safeguard

**Musical family:** **Until Help Arrives**

This family emphasizes protection and endurance rather than aggression.

1. **Safeguard Assigned** — target and objective reveal.
2. **Establish the Perimeter** — preparation and early positioning.
3. **Threats Approaching** — first hostile pressure.
4. **Hold the Line** — sustained objective danger.
5. **Extraction Inbound** — final protection interval.
6. **Safe Transfer** — successful evacuation or recovery.
7. **Regroup and Retry** — target loss or restart stinger.

Critical protection music may increase urgency but must not imitate alarms, spoken warnings, gunfire, Exact Block cues, boss tells, or other gameplay SFX.

## Random Operations arena integration

- The objective family selects the score structure; the seed never chooses arbitrary unrelated music.
- An unlocked arena may add one validated stage-specific instrumental color through a compatible authored overlay or premixed variant.
- Music and motifs from an uncompleted story stage remain unavailable so Random Operations cannot spoil locations, identities, or reveals.
- Arena overlays never replace the Defeat or Safeguard state meanings and never alter wave or objective logic.
- Solo and co-op use the same musical state graph. Co-op does not add a separate rhythm that could imply different simulation timing.

## Production and delivery rules

- Candidate Google Flow Music/Lyria output remains source material, not an automatically accepted final asset.
- Human editing establishes structure, compatible key and tempo, loop boundaries, transition stingers, boss states, and only useful intensity stems.
- Constrained devices receive browser-ready premixed state tracks with the same musical identity and state meaning.
- Music, ambience, dialogue, and low-latency gameplay SFX remain separate mixer categories.
- Essential player confirmations, defensive cues, objective warnings, projectile warnings, and boss telegraphs receive priority over music.
- Every candidate and final asset requires source, prompt, creation date, edit history, provenance, commercial-use verification, loudness normalization, browser format variants, and integrity metadata.
- Museum playback uses completed unlocked cues and localized titles without exposing unreached stage content.

## 2. Purpose and boundaries

Stage 7 occurs 47 months after Stage 2 and nine months after Stage 6. It must expose the Meridian Compact through lawful evidence, protect witnesses and uninvolved workers, complete Tomas’s renunciation without pardoning him, and establish immediate grounds for Stage 8.

The stage must not reveal the Compact before authentication, make Tomas a fighter or substitute witness, force family forgiveness, treat ordinary Forum staff as enemies, permit civilian death or evidence loss, add permanent weapons, change the combat engine, require co-op, or enable Story Automatic Highlights.

## 3. Six-Link Ledger Exposure Route

Target duration is 48–56 minutes first clear and 33–41 minutes replay. There are nine mandatory encounters plus Silas, two forward-reconnecting branches, and three safe review transitions.

1. **Yunzhou Council Forward Office:** briefing, Tomas handover, configuration.
2. **Shell Consultancy Office:** witnesses and Damon Acquisition Ledger.
3. **Freight Service Corridor:** bounded electric cargo-bike interception.
4. **Regional Freight Exchange:** workers and Rhea Operations Register.
5. **Meridian Forum Public Wing:** employee evacuation and Strategy Index.
6. **Forum Branch Archive:** Han Memorandum, authentication, Silas.

Active links stream without conventional loading. Missed interceptions may worsen condition or rank but never canonical recovery.

## 4. Opening and roster

The 25–35-second skippable **Converging Testimony / 汇聚的证言 / Zusammenlaufende Zeugnisse** shows locked consultancy records, diverted freight, Tomas arriving unarmed with the annex, and Wayfarer witness calls without naming the Compact.

Selectable heroes are Mountain Guardian, River Scout, Wind Sentinel, Fire Warden, and Kael Rell. Solo selects one; co-op selects two unique heroes. Unselected Kael appears only in support scenes. Soren and Chen Yue are non-playable Story allies and retain approved Mission Mode eligibility.

## 5. Authenticated Three-Ledger Chain

- **Damon Acquisition Ledger:** shell contracts, concealed subsidies, procurement cutouts, asset seizure.
- **Rhea Operations Register:** deployments, intimidation, cleanup, freight diversion.
- **Han Strategy Memorandum:** strategic intent, targets, two-of-three steering approval.

Lifecycle: `Unlocated → Identified → Under Erasure → Stabilized → Secured → Authenticated`.

Integrity: **Preserved → Reconstructed → Essential Core**. Each completed warned erasure lowers one step. An attempted loss below Essential Core restores the checkpoint. Safe review compares IDs, timestamps, signatures, routing, and custody without timed reading or guessing.

All three are indispensable. Tomas and earlier clues corroborate only. Authentication reveals the Meridian Compact, Han/Rhea/Damon roles, approval model, and Stage 8 grounds. Integrity affects performance and Museum detail, never reveal or access.

## 6. Protected Transfer

Mandatory groups are consultancy auditors/staff, freight dispatchers/workers, and Forum archivists/public-service employees.

Lifecycle: `Sheltered → Route Authorized → Protected Transfer → Council Safe`.

Conditions are **Protected**, **Disrupted**, and **Emergency Extracted**. Heroes clear/access/defend routes; Wayfarer/Council perform protected handoff. There is no follower escort. People have no Health, death, grab, shield, or environmental state. Witness Grabbers target route controls, not bodies.

Zero completed hostile route-control actions yields Protected. The first completed action yields Disrupted and the authored alternate route. The second yields Emergency Extracted and the slower protected emergency exit. Interrupted actions do not downgrade, and further completions cannot worsen the group or farm score. Council Safe permanently locks the earned condition. Solo/co-op thresholds are identical.

Mandatory testimony survives every condition. Solo permits one total evidence/civilian pressure; co-op two staggered separate reachable targets. Safe groups lock permanently.

## 7. Mandatory encounters

### 7.1 Open the Safe Route

Clear reception/corridor/safety lobby, release two doors, protect consultancy transfer, and introduce Suppressor, Grabber, and Escort. Workplace property is protected; cleanup equipment is legal.

### 7.2 The Acquisition Ledger

Locate Damon’s physical ledger, stabilize signed token/mirror, defend warned erasure, and secure custody. Shutters have manual release; cleanup partitions/consoles are legal; lawful cabinets are protected. Safe review matches Tomas’s fragment without revealing the organization.

### 7.3 Intercept the Cleanup Convoy

A 2–3-minute evacuated corridor uses one finite-Stability cargo bike solo or per co-op hero, safe/voluntary dismount, and equivalent on-foot path. Pressure Gates are Break the Screen, Clear the Barrier, and Stop the Credential Transfer. Three successes deny setup, two permit one barrier, zero/one permit reinforced shutters plus one ordinary slot within cap. Courier arrival is fixed; results affect opening/score only. Bikes cannot ram people, explode, carry evidence, or persist.

### 7.4 Hold the Dispatch Floor

Clear three freight lanes, restore/defend worker transfer, and introduce Technology Disabler. Corridor results alter only the declared setup.

### 7.5 Orders in Transit

Secure Rhea’s signed command terminal, dispatch seals, and route-history mirror. Deterministic lifts/carts provide safe cover and tagged redirects without free driving or protected harm. Review proves centralized command but not steering identity.

### 7.6 Separate the Public Wing

Council non-color/behavior markers distinguish cleanup personnel. Clear three evacuation routes and prevent false redirection. Ordinary Forum security withdraws, surrenders, or assists and is never hostile by employment.

### 7.7 The Strategy Index

Protect the public catalogue, recover non-graded Han locator/access metadata, restore archive lift, and prevent handshake corruption. Completed interference forces a longer manual route/time debit, never loss.

### 7.8 The Hidden Ledger

Secure Han’s signed policy folio, approval key, and meeting mirror under the shared integrity model.

### 7.9 Hold the Three Keys

Defend three authentication nodes using records held in documented custody. Hostile completion resets only one node progress and adds one efficiency incident; it cannot lower secured integrity. Authentication triggers the reveal. Silas enters immediately with no ordinary wave.

## 8. Council Field Support Robot

One shared robot is available at marked points in exactly The Acquisition Ledger, Hold the Dispatch Floor, The Strategy Index, The Hidden Ledger, and the Silas encounter. Each has one independent allocation. An ordinary encounter selects:

- **Mobile Cover:** temporary non-damaging barrier.
- **Objective Interference:** pauses, never cancels, one hostile channel.
- **Reserve Delay:** delays, never deletes, one ordinary reserve entry.

One effect may run. First pre-activation disruption retracts with one cooldown retry; second or success consumes. Disablers suppress temporarily only. Difficulty cannot weaken it. Complete state persists. It cannot damage, combo, handle evidence, complete/open objectives, revive, capture, auto-protect, or collect. Every opportunity has an unassisted solution.

During Silas, Mobile Cover remains available and Objective Interference may pause one active Last Redaction step without cancelling or completing it. Reserve Delay is Not Applicable. One boss allocation spans every phase, never refreshes, restores from exact pre-boss state, and may have its active effect visibly suppressed without losing unused availability.

## 9. Weapons and environment

Each is Protected at zero avoidable player incidents, Disturbed at one/two, and Damaged at three-plus. Enemy/required/accessibility/cleanup-owned/scripted damage is exempt. People, evidence, indispensable controls/history/routes are immune. Shared collision cooldown, diminishing control, launch limits, elite resistance, and Combo Breakers apply.

Attribution is exclusive: Consultancy/Public contains only lawful Shell Office doors, desks, cabinets, belongings, access equipment, and fixtures; Freight Safety contains only Corridor/Exchange rails, lawful barriers, worker platforms, maintenance controls, non-cleanup pallets, and automatic carts; Forum/Archive contains only public exhibits, community records, access systems, archive shelves, protected controls, and supplemental archive material. Cleanup-tagged objects have no ledger. One authored damage cluster counts once; cross-object impact attributes once to its originating cluster. No asset can affect two ledgers, and attribution/consumed-cluster state persists.

## 11. Boss — Silas Kern

**Silas Kern — The Erasure Chief / 西拉斯·克恩——抹除主管 / Silas Kern – Leiter der Spurenvernichtung**, 44, is Rhea’s direct cleanup commander, not a steering leader.

**Controlled Access:** complete baton kit and finite projection-shield Guard with legal flank/throw/Exact Block/environment/pressure counters.

**Suppression Geometry:** after shield break and Resolve threshold, three fixed emitters create one warned lane solo or two staggered safe-path lanes co-op. Silas may visibly suppress robot effects. Emitters cannot harm evidence or critical controls.

**Last Redaction:** one sequence attempts Seal Supplemental Archive → Divert Emergency Power → Trigger Purge. Each success adds one Archive Preservation Incident affecting supplemental Museum/archive condition only. Interrupted steps stagger; full completion triggers emergency isolation. Either result enters no-reset **Closed File** baton/unarmed combat. No retry, shield/emitter recovery, adds, or replenishment.

Zero Resolve yields non-QTE disarm, medical assessment, restraint, and live custody. Silas confirms Rhea’s authority without replacing proof or revealing Stage 8 tactics.

## 12. Tomas outcome

After independent proof, Tomas explicitly admits financing/planning armed village raids; manipulating Marek, Garran, Kael, and other intermediaries through incomplete or false accounts; participating in kidnapping/ransom/leverage conspiracy; defrauding Xu Lian's family and exploiting concealed custody procedures to control Kael's upbringing; building a revenge network that endangered unrelated people; and withholding the Stage 6 annex as leverage after abandonment. He ends collective punishment, submits Compact-linked claims to lawful restitution review, cooperates with individual investigations, and states childhood injustice does not excuse adult choices. No pardon, sentence reduction, restored wealth, forgiveness, or family contact is guaranteed. Kael and Xu Lian retain contact/forgiveness agency; Kael’s alliance is independent. Tomas remains a fixed noncombatant custody outcome and learns the Compact identity only after proof.

## 14. Solo/co-op equivalence

Both modes share route, content, evidence, conditions, gates, robot, resources, and outcome. Solo uses one hero/channel/bike with compensated timing. Co-op uses two unique heroes, at most two staggered separate channels, and two bikes without extra gates/objectives. Scaling adds coverage, not Health.

Camera/regroup prevents splits. Partner down extends only the later objective window. Separate normalized records and informational contributions apply; nothing requires co-op.

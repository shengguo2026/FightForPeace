# Fight for Peace - Stage 3 Feature and Story-Line Decisions

**Status:** Approved design record  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-01  
**Stage title:** *Shadows over the Herb Mountain* / *药山阴影* / *Schatten über dem Kräuterberg*  
**Scope:** The Stage 3 story, route, characters, rescue structure, content baselines, progression additions, and transition to Stage 4.

This document packages the approved Stage 3 decisions from `story_line.md` into a stage-focused reference. It extends the shared combat and campaign rules in `Features_Decisions.md` and the main game specification. It does not revise the finalized stories or feature sets of Stages 1 and 2.

## Decision labels

- **Confirmed** - approved Stage 3 direction.
- **Production baseline** - approved starting quantity or composition that may change through validated tuning without changing the feature model.
- **Deferred** - production work still required; not an unresolved product direction.

## 1. Stage identity and purpose

- **Confirmed:** Stage 3 occurs approximately 18 months after Stage 2.
- **Confirmed:** Its complete local conflict is a mountain rescue: villagers gathering a rare fictional research plant are kidnapped for ransom and leverage by an organized bandit group.
- **Confirmed:** The village patrol rescues every captive, secures enough documented material for research to continue, captures the bandit chief alive, and discovers evidence of renewed intermediary support.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas Rell returns as a physically present noncombatant strategist. He is dangerous because of planning, money, intelligence, contacts, and manipulation, not martial ability.
- **Confirmed:** Stage 3 adds no new selectable hero. Mountain Guardian, River Scout, Wind Sentinel, and Fire Warden are available, and duplicate heroes remain forbidden.
- **Confirmed:** The stage expands wilderness rescue, mounted traversal, environmental combat, hostages, optional branches, difficulty-specific enemies, Museum content, and campaign mystery without replacing the shared fighting engine.
- **Confirmed:** Stage 3 visibly advances clothing, communications, mapping, medical equipment, and sample storage. Robots and advanced combat wearables do not appear yet.

## 2. Scientific catalyst and safeguards

- **Confirmed:** Researchers continue controlled study of Tomas's formula and source notes after Stage 2.
- **Confirmed:** A refined fictional candidate preparation shows unusually strong control of selected tumor-cell growth in laboratory mice.
- **Confirmed:** This is a preclinical result only. It is not proof of a human cure, an approved medicine, or a combat enhancement.
- **Confirmed:** International medical institutions recognize the result and provide a grant for replication, toxicity research, conservation, provenance review, and possible controlled cultivation.
- **Confirmed:** The candidate requires a wholly fictional plant, **Silvervein Moonbell / 银脉月铃草 / Silberader-Mondglocke**.
- **Confirmed:** The plant is a small blue-violet mountain species with silver-veined leaves found in cool mist pockets on shaded high-altitude ledges. It is rare but not magical or inherently curative.
- **Confirmed:** Its invented **MV-3** cofactor stabilizes the fictional candidate sufficiently to improve consistency in the mouse research. MV-3 has no independent curative role.
- **Confirmed:** Story, Guide, Museum, cinematic, and item text may not provide real ingredients, extraction steps, preparation instructions, dosages, safety values, or an actionable biological mechanism.
- **Confirmed:** Silvervein Moonbell never becomes a recovery item, permanent stat increase, purchased move component, or combat power-up.

## 3. Research governance

- **Confirmed:** The Silvervein Moonbell habitat belongs to the Silvermist ecosystem rather than automatically becoming village property.
- **Confirmed:** Qinglan is a credited conservation and field-research partner.
- **Confirmed:** Collection requires community consent, limited sampling, habitat monitoring, and documented provenance.
- **Confirmed:** Funders gain no automatic ownership of the formula, habitat, village data, or future treatment rights.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas's earlier contribution remains documented but grants him neither sole ownership nor a right to seize later independent work.
- **Confirmed:** Field collection pauses after the kidnapping until the route is secured and Qinglan agrees that sampling may resume.
- **Confirmed:** Institutional backups ensure that kidnapping Ren Xia cannot transfer, erase, or monopolize the research.
- **Confirmed:** Stage 3 adds no hostile institution, ownership lawsuit, corporate antagonist, or artificial bureaucratic obstruction. Human trials, licensing, access, and benefit-sharing remain later peaceful-development questions.

## 4. Silvermist Highlands and route

- **Confirmed:** Stage 3 takes place in the **Silvermist Highlands / 银雾岭 / Silbernebel-Hochland**, above the river valley.
- **Confirmed:** Mountain waterfalls create the cool shaded mist pockets required by Silvervein Moonbell.
- **Confirmed:** The route is:

  `village briefing -> Lower Switchback -> Qinglan Hamlet -> Moonbell Basin -> Echo Quarry -> Broken Ridge Camp -> High Winch Terrace`

### 4.1 Lower Switchback

- **Confirmed:** One horse per active hero introduces mountain riding safely before an ambush trail turns the sequence into a pursuit.
- **Confirmed:** Abandoned gathering equipment and evidence establish the kidnapping without providing an implausibly complete camp map.

### 4.2 Qinglan Hamlet

- **Confirmed:** **Qinglan Hamlet / 青岚村 / Dorf Qinglan** is a modern cliffside community supporting gathering, trail maintenance, conservation, rescue, and horse handling.
- **Confirmed:** Bandits damage its communications but do not occupy the whole settlement.
- **Confirmed:** Residents use practical weather protection, locally produced woven details, trail ledgers, conservation zones, radios, rescue bells, weather signals, and cooperative horse shelters.
- **Confirmed:** Qinglan is neither mystical nor the keeper of a secret cure. Controlled scientific study remains the researchers' responsibility.

### 4.3 Moonbell Basin

- **Confirmed:** **Moonbell Basin / 月铃谷 / Mondglockental** is the protected north-facing habitat.
- **Confirmed:** The heroes secure abandoned documented samples and rescue Gao Wen and Tao Min without indiscriminate harvesting.

### 4.4 Echo Quarry

- **Confirmed:** **Echo Quarry / 回声石场 / Echosteinbruch** is a disused quarry containing stable service tunnels, timber scaffolds, mine carts, transport equipment, and an old herbalist shelter.
- **Confirmed:** The heroes rescue An Bo here. He helps calm the recovered horses.
- **Confirmed:** An old service passage also supports Tomas's prepared escape.

### 4.5 Broken Ridge Camp and High Winch Terrace

- **Confirmed:** **Broken Ridge Camp / 断脊营地 / Lager am Gebrochenen Grat** occupies a former quarry logistics site controlling both major passes.
- **Confirmed:** Captives are held in separated, reachable work and storage enclosures rather than one convenient cage.
- **Confirmed:** Ren Xia is rescued before the boss approach.
- **Confirmed:** **High Winch Terrace / 高索台 / Hochseil-Terrasse** is a broad former loading platform with retaining walls, cargo rails, winches, breakable timber cover, and safe boundaries.
- **Confirmed:** Environmental attacks are tactical; victory never requires a lethal cliff fall.

## 5. Expedition survivors and captives

Two expedition members return to raise the alarm:

- **Luo Fen / 罗芬**, age 44, is the trusted gathering coordinator sent back with the ransom demand.
- **Deng Hao / 邓浩**, age 32, is a clinic field medic with a non-life-threatening injury who documents the ambush.

Four people remain captive:

- **Gao Wen / 高文**, age 52, Qinglan's trail and conservation steward, has an injured ankle and keeps Tao calm.
- **Tao Min / 陶敏**, age 22, apprentice gatherer, observes bandit movements and later recalls the route toward the camp.
- **An Bo / 安博**, age 35, horse handler and rescue volunteer, is forced to maintain captured horses and quarry transport equipment.
- **Ren Xia / 任霞**, age 41, village research liaison, is responsible for sample provenance and refuses to authenticate exaggerated medical claims or reveal preparation information.

### 5.1 Rescue rules

- **Confirmed:** All four rescues are mandatory, and every captive survives canonically.
- **Confirmed:** There is no hidden execution timer.
- **Confirmed:** Each rescue group receives a visible **Secure**, **Strained**, or **Critical** condition grade based on delay, environmental damage, and protection performance.
- **Confirmed:** Condition changes medical presentation, score, recognition, and epilogue details. It never causes death, blocks completion, or changes the canonical story.
- **Confirmed:** Freed captives follow short authored routes to protected shelters. Stage 3 does not become a long escort mission.
- **Confirmed:** Captives provide contextual knowledge but never become combatants.

## 6. Tomas Rell's role

- **Confirmed:** Tomas has joined Garran's bandits after his expulsion. He uses wealth, contacts, village knowledge, business skills, radios, maps, and logistics to organize a larger operation.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas believes he is buying equipment and support for his own revenge through brokers related to the earlier hidden network. He does not know the sponsor's identity.
- **Confirmed:** The sponsor treats Tomas as a deniable instrument for pressuring and studying the village. Both sides believe they are exploiting the other.
- **Confirmed:** Early areas show evidence of organized planning before Tomas is revealed directly.
- **Confirmed:** At Moonbell Basin, an intercepted open-radio exchange confirms Tomas's recognizable voice and his role in moving Ren Xia.
- **Confirmed:** At Broken Ridge Camp, the heroes see Tomas in an elevated planning cabin questioning Ren. She refuses to validate exaggerated claims or describe preparation methods.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas begins withdrawing before the heroes can reach him, activates a prearranged signal, takes only portable records, uses a quarry cart through the service passage, and transfers to a waiting horse.
- **Confirmed:** The accessible player route leads to Ren, while an ordinary gate and damaged rail close Tomas's route. The player receives no false chase objective and loses no reward for prioritizing rescue.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas reaches a lowland shelter and contacts a former commercial broker in the epilogue, beginning the Stage 4 transition.

## 7. Mandatory sponsor clue

- **Confirmed:** The Stage 3 clue comes from Garran's private supply case, not from Tomas carelessly abandoning decisive evidence.
- **Confirmed:** Garran secretly retained a damaged payment invoice as leverage against Tomas.
- **Confirmed:** After Garran's capture, his key and testimony lead to the case.
- **Confirmed:** A readable procurement batch reference links the bandits to the same route family seen in Stages 1 and 2 while identifying only the regional procurement cutout.
- **Confirmed:** The clue is mandatory, checkpoint-safe, impossible to destroy or miss, and does not reveal the sponsor's identity.

## 8. Garran Voss - The Ridge Wolf

- **Confirmed:** The boss is **Garran Voss - The Ridge Wolf / 加兰·沃斯 - 岭狼 / Garran Voss - Der Gratwolf**, age 46.
- **Confirmed:** Garran is a former quarry transport foreman who used intimidation, stolen cargo, and illegal tolls before Tomas helped expand his band.
- **Confirmed:** His motives are ransom, route control, and wealth rather than Tomas's family revenge.
- **Confirmed:** Garran and Tomas cooperate while distrusting and regarding one another as disposable.
- **Confirmed:** Garran is broad, weathered, and powerful, but not a giant armored brute. He wears layered charcoal and ochre work clothing, a dull-red reinforced vest, quarry gloves, a rope harness, heavy trail boots, and a compact radio.
- **Confirmed:** He has no military uniform, wolf costume, sponsor emblem, or futuristic armor.
- **Confirmed:** His style is **Ridge Hook Wrestling**, built around a blunt **Quarry Hookstaff**.

### 8.1 Boss phases

1. **Hookstaff Control:** long sweeps, guarded thrusts, hooked pulls, low attacks, and short descending strikes.
2. **Clinch and Cast:** collar ties, shoulder throws, trips, body blows, and bounded ground follow-ups after staff damage or disarm.
3. **Winch Pressure:** telegraphed winch actions reposition cover and cargo and can be avoided, interrupted, or redirected.

- **Confirmed:** The Hookstaff follows normal durability and disarm rules and becomes interactable only through the authored boss disarm.
- **Confirmed:** Garran uses the shared Defense Bar, Exact Block, Counter, defense-consuming Combo Breaker, combo scaling, anti-infinite protection, and punishable recovery.
- **Confirmed:** In co-op, the second hero can interrupt hooked grabs. No sequence creates an unbreakable single-player lock.
- **Confirmed:** Garran is defeated through disarm, Resolve break, restraint, medical assessment, and live capture.

## 9. Playable roster and co-op

- **Confirmed:** Mountain Guardian, River Scout, Wind Sentinel, and Fire Warden are selectable.
- **Confirmed:** Every hero is solo-complete under the shared combat rules.
- **Confirmed:** Hot-seat and local-Wi-Fi co-op use unique hero selections; duplicates remain prohibited because each hero is a unique person in the story.
- **Confirmed:** Hero choice may change authored dialogue and presentation but cannot change established facts, rescues, evidence, or the canonical ending.
- **Confirmed:** Existing cooperative juggle, handoff, rescue, environmental redirect, and Combo Breaker rules remain active.

## 10. Authored route and encounter baseline

- **Production baseline:** First Story completion targets 40-50 minutes. An experienced replay targets 25-35 minutes.
- **Production baseline:** The route contains 12 mandatory combat encounters, Garran's boss encounter, and two optional encounters.
- **Production baseline:** Encounter distribution is:
  - Lower Switchback: 2 mandatory.
  - Qinglan Hamlet: 2 mandatory plus the Signal Hut branch.
  - Moonbell Basin: 2 mandatory.
  - Echo Quarry: 3 mandatory plus the Herbalist Shelter branch.
  - Broken Ridge Camp: 3 mandatory.
  - High Winch Terrace: Garran.
- **Confirmed:** Counts are playtest baselines. Tuning may change population and composition without changing the route, rescue order, content classes, or clue continuity.

## 11. Mounts

- **Confirmed:** Mountain horses are the Stage 3 mount and receive no military equipment.
- **Production baseline:** One horse per active hero is available at Lower Switchback.
- **Production baseline:** One replacement per active hero becomes available after An Bo is rescued.
- **Confirmed:** Mounted play appears in the opening and a quarry-to-camp pursuit rather than dominating the whole stage.
- **Confirmed:** A horse retreats safely at zero Stability and never dies.
- **Confirmed:** Existing mounted input, Stability, co-op, checkpoint, accessibility, and cheat rules remain authoritative.

## 12. Pickable weapons

Medium baseline:

- 2 Hiking Staffs.
- 1 main-route Wood Axe.
- 2 Quarry Hammers.
- 1 bandit revolver with five remaining rounds.
- Garran's Quarry Hookstaff, available only through the boss disarm.

- **Confirmed:** All weapons are acquired during play from the environment or enemies. None can be equipped before the stage.
- **Confirmed:** Weapons retain the shared `J` pickup, use, carry, handoff, throw, durability, ammunition, checkpoint, and deterministic-ownership rules.
- **Production baseline:** Easy adds one Staff and one Hammer. Extreme and Ultimate remove one duplicate Staff without removing an entire weapon class.
- **Confirmed:** The revolver remains finite, disposable, readable, and incapable of creating a stun loop.

## 13. Recovery items

Medium baseline:

- 3 Life recovery items.
- 2 Defense recovery items.
- 2 Momentum recovery items.
- 1 rare Mountain Camp Meal in the optional Herbalist Shelter.

The Stage 3 food family is:

- **Trail rice parcel** - Life category.
- **Warm vegetable broth** - Defense category.
- **Spiced seed cake** - Momentum category.
- **Mountain camp meal** - scarce balanced recovery.

- **Production baseline:** Easy adds three common items; Hard removes one; Extreme removes two; Ultimate removes three. The rare optional meal remains available on every difficulty.
- **Confirmed:** Food uses the existing `J` pickup, carry, consume, and throw rules.
- **Confirmed:** Food effects are gameplay abstractions rather than medical claims.

## 14. Enemy roster and difficulty

Core roles:

- **Trail Ambusher:** mobile flanker using terrain and readable approach paths.
- **Quarry Bruiser:** slower pressure role with committed heavy actions.
- **Rope Trapper:** control role with visible setup, interruptible restraint, and legal escape opportunities.
- **Mounted Raider:** horse-based pressure using the shared mount rules.

Difficulty specialists:

- **Ridge Stalker:** Extreme specialist using visible feints and punishable commits.
- **Pack Captain:** Ultimate specialist coordinating readable flanks that can be disrupted.

Initial solo populations:

| Difficulty | Baseline population and identity |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | Approximately 22 ordinary enemies and beginner-readable formations. |
| Medium | Approximately 30 enemies and the canonical role mix. |
| Hard | Approximately 34 enemies with stronger role combinations. |
| Extreme | Approximately 36 enemies including Ridge Stalkers. |
| Ultimate | Approximately 40 enemies including Ridge Stalkers and Pack Captains. |

- **Production baseline:** Co-op targets roughly 35 percent more total threat rather than double counts.
- **Confirmed:** Simultaneous-pressure caps, telegraphs, finite defensive resources, defense-consuming Combo Breakers, scaling, recovery, and anti-infinite rules remain mandatory at every difficulty.
- **Confirmed:** Higher difficulty emphasizes composition, cooperation, timing, and objective pressure rather than health and damage alone.

## 15. Environmental combat

- **Confirmed:** Stage 3 uses the shared authored deterministic environmental system.
- **Confirmed:** Quarry scaffolds, carts, loose stone, timber barriers, retaining structures, rails, winches, cover, and cargo provide the principal interactions.
- **Confirmed:** Eligible impacts can create tactical damage, knockdown, repositioning, redirection, or interactive debris only through authored profiles.
- **Confirmed:** Garran's Winch Pressure uses clear warnings and can be avoided, interrupted, or redirected.
- **Confirmed:** Environmental actions cannot create infinite bounces, unavoidable hostage damage, lethal cliff requirements, or destruction of mandatory evidence.
- **Confirmed:** Functional warnings survive reduced effects and adaptive quality settings.

## 16. Optional branches, Museum, and achievements

### 16.1 Signal Hut

- **Confirmed:** The heroes restore Qinglan's cliffside signal hut and rescue its trapped operator.

### 16.2 Herbalist Shelter

- **Confirmed:** The heroes search the abandoned shelter for missing gathering equipment.
- **Confirmed:** The branch contains the rare Mountain Camp Meal.

### 16.3 Rewards and clues

- **Confirmed:** Completing both branches grants a Stage 3 achievement and upgrades the Museum presentation.
- **Confirmed:** The Museum receives a non-actionable illustrated mountain-ecology record.
- **Confirmed:** Optional branches provide bounded score, Credits, records, recognition, and discoveries but no mandatory story fact or combat power.
- **Confirmed:** Missed discoveries remain available through replay.
- **Confirmed:** Locked achievement and Museum entries display localized, spoiler-safe unlock clues through hover, keyboard or gamepad focus, click or confirmation, touch, and accessibility labels.
- **Deferred:** Exact achievement names, thresholds, Credit values, score values, and catalogue size remain production data.

## 17. Store and clothing progression

- **Confirmed:** Each of the four available heroes receives a character-specific **Mountain Rescue Outfit** and **Highland Gathering Festival Attire**.
- **Confirmed:** These are meaningful visual redesigns rather than palette swaps, remain visual-only, and do not alter speed, defense, damage, moves, or difficulty eligibility.
- **Confirmed:** The Stage Service and Heritage outfits remain permanently purchasable after their reveal condition and are eligible in Standard Loadouts.
- **Confirmed:** Each hero also receives a free canonical default-clothing evolution for Stage 3 so story modernization is not locked behind the Store.
- **Confirmed:** Stage 3 adds no advanced combat wearable or robot equipment.
- **Deferred:** Character-specific final cuts, colors, prices, unlock wording, model sheets, and animation adaptation remain production work.

## 18. Presentation, localization, and music

- **Confirmed:** English is the default language, with complete Simplified Chinese and German localization.
- **Confirmed:** Generated story imagery and video contain no embedded text. Titles, subtitles, captions, and UI are runtime overlays.
- **Confirmed:** Every generated clip has a still-art fallback and transcript. Essential story information cannot depend on video, audio, color, high motion, or a generated voice.
- **Confirmed:** Silent or clean-source clips may be combined with localized subtitles, narration, music, ambience, and SFX during implementation.
- **Confirmed:** Flow prompts follow the established production lessons: favor small motion already supported by the frame, name each prop hand and gaze target, define container contents and exclusions, verify the prompt against the still, and redesign the action after two failures.
- **Confirmed:** The Stage 3 musical family is **Mist and Resolve**:
  1. Silver Veins in Morning Mist.
  2. The Empty Gathering Trail.
  3. Hooves above the Ravine.
  4. Quarry of Echoes.
  5. Captives in the High Camp.
  6. Chief of the Broken Ridge.
  7. Paths through the Mist.
- **Confirmed:** Music follows the campaign's Authored Adaptive Score, remains separate from SFX and ambience, cannot mask combat warnings, and uses the provenance and commercial-rights controls in `docs/production/campaign_music_direction.md`.
- **Deferred:** Final script, shot list, clip count, dialogue, voice casting, subtitle timing, score masters, SFX, and asset production.

## 19. Checkpoints and canonical ending

Five checkpoint payloads cover:

1. Stage departure.
2. Qinglan arrival after the mounted introduction.
3. Gao Wen and Tao Min rescued.
4. An Bo rescued.
5. Ren Xia rescued immediately before Garran.

- **Confirmed:** Restore preserves rescue grades, optional branches, consumed resources, weapon ownership and condition, revolver ammunition, horse state, opened containers, and persistent environmental damage.
- **Confirmed:** Garran's private case appears only after his capture; the clue cannot be missed, destroyed, duplicated, or lost through checkpoint restoration.
- **Confirmed:** The canonical outcome is: all captives survive, enough documented herb material is recovered, Garran is captured alive, Tomas escapes, and the damaged invoice begins the Stage 4 investigation.
- **Confirmed:** Performance affects rank, condition presentation, medical details, resources, optional rewards, achievements, and epilogue inserts. It does not create a divergent campaign ending.

## 20. Shared Guide, Academy, and cheat coverage

- **Confirmed:** Stage 3 inherits the localized Guide, Academy, Move List, My Combos, Combat Lab, and accessibility architecture.
- **Confirmed:** Teaching additions cover mountain-horse control, hostage-condition feedback, short protected routes, Stage 3 weapons, Rope Trapper escape, Ridge Stalker feints, Pack Captain coordination, and Garran's Hookstaff and winch counterplay.
- **Confirmed:** Stage 3 introduces no separate required cheat code. Existing documented modifiers apply where legal, including `STEADYRIDE` for mounted Stability.
- **Confirmed:** Cheat-Assisted runs may complete the story and unlock later difficulties under the shared rules, but cannot award score, Credits, mastery achievements, grades, or competitive records.

## 21. Stage evolution comparison

| Area | Stage 1 | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local conflict | Restore the seized river system. | Protect the prosperous greenhouse system from infiltration and raid. | Rescue kidnapped gatherers and secure the mountain route. |
| Selectable heroes | Mountain Guardian and River Scout. | Adds Wind Sentinel and Fire Warden. | Uses all four established heroes; no new debut. |
| Main setting | River, bridge, mill, canal, gate. | Modernizing village and greenhouse complex. | Highland road, Qinglan, protected basin, quarry, bandit camp. |
| Mount identity | Representative architecture only; no full stage mount focus. | Mid-class motorbikes. | Mountain horses in two bounded sections. |
| Weapon identity | Workstaff and handwheel representative slice. | Modern finite-ammunition weapons and service tools. | Hiking staff, wood axe, quarry hammer, limited revolver, boss Hookstaff. |
| Environment | Representative environmental slice. | Preservation-aware living systems. | Terrain and quarry interactions with rescue-safe boundaries. |
| Difficulty evolution | Shared foundation and unlock ladder. | Advanced formations and Stage 2 specialists. | Ridge Stalker on Extreme and Pack Captain on Ultimate. |
| Optional content | Initial stage discoveries. | Three greenhouse branches and expandable Museum. | Two mountain branches and ecology record. |
| Story clue | First unknown-sponsor order and seal evidence. | Shared seal and related order-code route to abandoned depot. | Damaged invoice and procurement batch reference identify a regional cutout. |
| Canonical result | Water restored. | Village protected, Tomas expelled, formula quarantined. | Captives rescued, Garran captured, Tomas escapes, Stage 4 inquiry begins. |

Stage 3 expands the campaign without retroactively changing Stages 1 or 2. Previous stages are revised only for serious contradictions or defects, not to erase the player's sense of progression.

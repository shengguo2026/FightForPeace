# Fight for Peace — Stage 2 Feature and Story-Line Decisions

**Status:** Living refinement document  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-24  
**Scope:** The three-year transition after Stage 1, the Prosperity Interlude, and the current Stage 2 story and feature direction.

This document records decisions approved during refinement. It extends the existing game specification without replacing the confirmed core combat, profile, localization, accessibility, co-op, difficulty, save, achievement, weapon, environmental, and technical rules.

## Decision labels

- **Confirmed** — approved direction for Stage 2.
- **Provisional** — useful working target requiring later content design or Combat Lab validation.
- **Unresolved** — intentionally deferred for later refinement.

## 1. Transition from Stage 1

- **Confirmed:** Approximately three years pass between restoration of the river at the end of Stage 1 and the beginning of Stage 2.
- **Confirmed:** The restored water supply creates lasting prosperity rather than serving only as a short ending celebration.
- **Confirmed:** The village constructs its own reservoirs, rainwater storage, expanded irrigation, and related reserves so that it is no longer completely dependent on a single river source.
- **Confirmed:** Villagers work collectively and use the recovered water intelligently to improve food security, trade, education, health care, infrastructure, and long-term resilience.
- **Confirmed:** New homes, a school, a hospital or expanded clinic, workshops, greenhouses, and a civic or local-government headquarters visibly transform the village while preserving its established cultural identity.
- **Confirmed:** A meaningful portion of the village's new wealth is reinvested into education, health care, engineering, agriculture, civic services, and the training of future protectors.
- **Confirmed:** Villagers and returning heroes wear better-tailored, cleaner, more colorful, and more durable clothing after the three-year development period. Existing heroes retain their recognizable silhouettes and identity.

## 2. Modern agriculture and aquaponics

- **Confirmed:** The village develops scientifically credible greenhouse agriculture using hydroponic and aquaponic techniques.
- **Confirmed:** The principal commercial arrangement uses connected but distinct fish or freshwater-prawn tanks, solid filtration, biological filtration, pumps, aeration, and hydroponic plant beds or channels in a recirculating water system.
- **Confirmed:** A smaller demonstration basin may show leafy plants above floating beds, roots extending into oxygenated water, fish swimming below, and freshwater prawns occupying protected lower areas.
- **Confirmed:** The system produces vegetables, crops, fish, freshwater prawns, and selected medicinal or aromatic plants.
- **Confirmed:** Medicinal crops and any derived preparation are scientifically tested by the village clinic rather than treated as automatically safe or effective.
- **Confirmed:** Agricultural infrastructure must visibly include circulation, filtration, aeration, monitoring, and maintenance rather than depicting a self-operating magical ecosystem.

## 3. Civil protection and peacekeeping

- **Confirmed:** Prosperity leads the village to establish a civil-protection and peacekeeping program rather than a conquering military force.
- **Confirmed:** The program trains selected young adults in rescue, first aid, evacuation, de-escalation, nonlethal restraint, modern martial arts, vehicle or mount handling, and supervised weapon safety.
- **Confirmed:** Members are publicly accountable community protectors. Their clothing uses village colors and cultural details instead of military camouflage.
- **Confirmed:** Protective equipment may include reinforced jackets, flexible torso protection, gloves, forearm guards, knee and shin protection, protective collars, helmets or eye protection for relevant duties, and ankle-supporting protective boots.
- **Confirmed:** Protection remains role-specific and does not turn every hero into the same armored soldier.

## 4. Prosperity Interlude

- **Confirmed:** A skippable **Prosperity Interlude** bridges the Stage 1 ending and Stage 2 introduction.
- **Confirmed:** The selected presentation is a **Hybrid Village Chronicle**: chronological development is shown through recurring villagers, future heroes, and recognizable locations rather than an impersonal infrastructure montage.
- **Confirmed:** The sequence communicates hard work, economic intelligence, scientific progress, community reinvestment, improved daily life, and the creation of civil protection.
- **Confirmed:** Quality takes precedence over generating the complete sequence in one day. Google Flow clips may be produced across multiple free-credit cycles and edited together.
- **Confirmed:** Individual Flow generations may be short, but multiple selected clips may be combined into a longer final cinematic.
- **Provisional:** Approximately 12–16 source clips may be generated and edited into a roughly 65–90-second interlude. Final duration follows pacing and quality rather than a rigid clip count.
- **Confirmed:** Codex will later generate continuity-controlled start-frame artwork for the Flow clips using a shared village map, architecture, palette, recurring characters, costume progression, and season/year continuity.
- **Confirmed:** Generated imagery and video contain no embedded language-dependent text.
- **Confirmed:** The interlude includes the following development chapters:
  1. restored water becoming long-term water security;
  2. reservoirs, irrigation, construction, and changing seasons;
  3. greenhouse and aquaponic construction;
  4. the wide modern-greenhouse establishment view;
  5. the visible plant-root, fish, and freshwater-prawn water cycle;
  6. vegetable, medicinal-plant, fish, and prawn harvests;
  7. reinvestment into homes, education, health care, workshops, and civic infrastructure;
  8. gradual clothing and quality-of-life improvement; and
  9. the civil-protection and peacekeeping program.
- **Confirmed:** The final civil-protection portion may feature the future Stage 2 heroes, but their full narrative introduction belongs to Stage 2.

## 5. Stage 2 story premise

### 5.1 The staged rescue

- **Confirmed:** During an outer agricultural-route patrol, the civil-protection guard unit discovers villains pursuing an injured old man.
- **Confirmed:** The guards intervene, rescue the old man, and drive the apparent pursuers away.
- **Confirmed:** The pursuit and injury are a coordinated deception. The old man is secretly allied with the people who appear to be chasing him.
- **Confirmed:** The old man remains a noncombatant infiltrator throughout the story. He does not become a martial-arts boss.

### 5.2 The offer

- **Confirmed:** The injured old man is taken to the village clinic or hospital and treated compassionately.
- **Confirmed:** He claims to possess a plant-based pharmaceutical technique capable of improving recovery while reducing harmful effects on the body.
- **Confirmed:** In gratitude for being rescued, he offers to share the technique with the village.
- **Confirmed:** The elder accepts custody of the proposal and allows the old man to recover under supervision.
- **Confirmed:** The old man has genuine technical knowledge and ultimately possesses a real secret formula; the technique is not merely an empty invention.
- **Confirmed:** The claimed technique does not function as a miraculous instant cure. Any use must be independently reproduced and validated by the village clinic and greenhouse researchers.

### 5.3 Recovery, tour, and suspicion

- **Confirmed:** The old man spends approximately two weeks recovering.
- **Confirmed:** After largely recovering, he requests a tour of the village and inspection of the greenhouse project so that he can supposedly prepare his work.
- **Confirmed:** During the next three days, he studies the village layout and prepares to contact his accomplices.
- **Confirmed:** The elder becomes suspicious because many of the old man's questions concern structures, roads, access routes, schedules, and logistics rather than agriculture or medicine.
- **Confirmed:** The elder quietly orders surveillance and prepares the village guard rather than publicly confronting the old man immediately.

### 5.4 Betrayal and partial success

- **Confirmed:** At night, the old man attempts to contact his outside accomplices and trigger the raid.
- **Confirmed:** Waiting guards reveal themselves when he acts.
- **Confirmed:** The infiltrator's partial success endangers only the greenhouse complex. Residential, educational, medical, and civic areas are not breached.
- **Confirmed:** A previously arranged secondary access, sabotage, or signal permits the raiders to threaten the greenhouse even though the elder prevents complete surprise.
- **Confirmed:** Researchers and civilians use the civil-protection plan to evacuate or reach protected positions.

### 5.5 Resolution

- **Confirmed:** The invaders are defeated. Surviving attackers may surrender or flee according to the game's established non-execution rules.
- **Confirmed:** The old man is captured alive.
- **Confirmed:** Fearing for his life, he hands over the genuine secret technical formula.
- **Confirmed:** The village does not execute defeated prisoners; the formula does not literally purchase his life.
- **Confirmed:** The elder accepts custody of the formula but does not forgive the betrayal or restore the old man's access.
- **Confirmed:** The old man is disarmed, documented, and permanently expelled from the village under guard.
- **Confirmed:** The clinic quarantines and validates the formula, investigates its origin, confirms safe preparation and dosage, and determines whether another inventor deserves credit before any gameplay or story benefit becomes available.
- **Confirmed:** The formula is genuine specialist knowledge covering the cultivation, extraction, preparation, and dosage control of selected medicinal plants. It may improve recovery and reduce adverse effects compared with existing preparations, but it is not a universal cure.
- **Confirmed:** The chief accepts the formula for independent investigation rather than treating it as proof of the infiltrator's honesty. Useful knowledge does not erase his conspiracy or earn forgiveness.
- **Confirmed:** Any in-game formula is fictionalized and non-actionable: story text, cinematics, records, and item descriptions do not provide real-world preparation instructions or medical dosing.

### 5.6 Infiltrator identity and presentation

- **Confirmed:** The infiltrator is **Tomas Rell**, an elderly former medicinal-crop and extraction specialist. He is a compromised genuine expert rather than a coerced innocent or a professional spy with stolen technical language.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas was formerly attached to a regional agricultural-research cooperative, where he helped cultivate medicinal plants and document extraction, preparation, and dosage controls. He contributed to the knowledge behind the formula but did not create it alone and retained an unauthorized copy after leaving.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas later sells his expertise to Marek Vane's network and joins the infiltration willingly for money, protection, and personal survival. He expects the prosperous village to be an easy target and believes the raiders will reward and protect him; his fear after capture overwhelms his loyalty to Marek.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas is in his late sixties, lean and weathered, with a neatly trimmed gray-white beard, observant eyes, a repaired traveling coat, a plant-sample satchel, and clinic bandages over genuine arranged injuries. His initial design reads as credible and vulnerable rather than visibly sinister; after exposure, a less frail posture and watchful calculating behavior reveal what was previously concealed.
- **Confirmed:** His voice begins gentle, educated, measured, and mildly breathless during recovery, using credible technical vocabulary. After exposure it becomes clipped, defensive, and bargaining. Accent is never used as a shortcut for dishonesty, and all spoken or subtitled material follows the approved localization and accessibility rules.
- **Confirmed:** In the post-boss resolution, Tomas provides the formula, source notes, and known provenance. The clinic seals them for independent validation, and the elder states that useful knowledge will be preserved and properly credited but cannot purchase innocence.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas receives necessary medical care and basic travel provisions before civil-protection guards escort him beyond the village boundary at dawn. His identity, conspiracy, evidence, and permanent exclusion are documented for any legitimate regional authority. He is neither forgiven nor celebrated, but he is not attacked, executed, or abandoned in immediate mortal danger.
- **Confirmed:** The unresolved authorship and cooperative history of the formula may support a later appearance by its true inventor, another contributor, or the former research cooperative without changing Tomas's responsibility for the Stage 2 conspiracy.
- **Deferred:** Final portrait artwork, costume details, localized name review, casting, vocal performance, precise dialogue, cinematic shot composition, and regional-record procedure remain production values.

### 5.7 Layered Hybrid Epilogue

- **Confirmed:** Stage 2 ends through a **Layered Hybrid Epilogue** rather than one uninterrupted movie or a mandatory post-boss exploration sequence. Short cinematic passages, still-art fallbacks, and concise in-engine scenes divide the resolution into replay-friendly chapters.
- **Confirmed:** The arena resolution first presents Marek's performance-sensitive surrender or safe incapacitation, secures the remaining raiders, removes immediate hazards, and confirms that critical greenhouse operations remain recoverable.
- **Confirmed:** The final Preservation Ledger then summarizes saved, damaged, and stabilized systems. Brief visual inserts reflect the actual run without changing the canonical defeat of the raid or creating a hidden bad ending.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas hands over the formula, source notes, and provenance information. The clinic seals this material for independent scientific validation rather than immediately treating it as proven medicine.
- **Confirmed:** The sponsor discovery combines the marked Stage 2 weapon crate, recognition of the Stage 1 supply seal by a returning founder, and the elder's comparison of the incomplete order-code structures and intermediary route.
- **Confirmed:** Justice is deliberately differentiated: Marek receives treatment under guard and remains detained for documented lawful transfer, while Tomas receives necessary care and basic provisions before permanent escorted expulsion at dawn.
- **Confirmed:** A community-recovery passage shows workers reunited, damaged areas stabilized or repaired, and surviving crops and aquatic systems according to the run's preservation result. It celebrates collective recovery rather than presenting prosperity as automatic or dependent on one hero.
- **Confirmed:** The forward mystery hook shows scouts reaching the intermediary depot after it has been deliberately abandoned, establishing an organized cover-up without revealing the sponsor.
- **Confirmed:** The closing results award eligible score, Credits, achievements, records, Museum discoveries, formula-research progress, and Sponsor Dossier entries. Cheat-Assisted rules and optional-content rules remain unchanged.
- **Confirmed:** All mandatory story facts remain identical across eligible performance variations. Surrender, preservation, optional branches, and discoveries change presentation details and recognition, not access to essential narrative information.
- **Deferred:** Exact scene durations, dialogue, shot list, performance-variation inserts, Google Flow generations, still frames, narration, localized subtitles, music, sound, transitions, and Museum chapter boundaries remain presentation-production work.

### 5.8 Regional Civil Protection Council

- **Confirmed:** Marek Vane's receiving authority is the **Regional Civil Protection Council**, a civilian mutual-aid institution formed by nearby villages and towns for serious cross-community crimes, emergency coordination, evidence preservation, neutral hearings, and documented prisoner transfer.
- **Confirmed:** The council is neither a military faction nor a replacement for local civil protection. The village retains responsibility for immediate safety, medical care, temporary custody, and evidence collection before requesting a verified transfer.
- **Confirmed:** Marek and eligible surviving captured raiders are transferred only after medical assessment and preparation of an evidence inventory, witness statements, weapon records, translated copies, and custody documentation.
- **Confirmed:** A small noncombat council escort verifies the record, accepts custody, and issues a visible receipt and case identifier. The village keeps certified copies for its own archive, continuing investigation, and Sponsor Dossier.
- **Confirmed:** Tomas is not transferred with Marek because his approved outcome remains permanent escorted expulsion. His identity, medical treatment, technical material, conspiracy evidence, and exclusion are nevertheless reported to the council so that nearby communities are not left uninformed.
- **Confirmed:** The council represents accountable regional cooperation, but it is not all-powerful and does not immediately solve the sponsor mystery. Its existence may support future allied-settlement, emergency, legal, or political stories without committing the campaign to a fully defined national government in Stage 2.
- **Confirmed:** Nothing in the Stage 2 presentation suggests that the council is secretly compromised or that lawful transfer was a mistake. Any later institutional conflict must preserve the reasonableness of the village's documented decision based on what was known at the time.
- **Deferred:** Final localized council name, emblem, escort clothing, transport design, custody forms, case-number format, legal terminology, named representatives, receiving location, and any later-stage role remain world-building and production values.

## 6. New playable heroes

- **Confirmed:** Stage 2 introduces two new playable heroes without removing the two founding heroes.
- **Confirmed:** The new heroes are an 18-year-old athletic sister and brother who are fraternal twins and members of the village civil-protection program.
- **Confirmed:** They form the canonical patrol pair that rescues the old man, giving both a personal relationship to the betrayal without treating their compassionate rescue as a mistake.
- **Confirmed:** The rescue patrol is presented cinematically rather than forcing the player to control the twins. The main greenhouse attack preserves normal hero selection and allows any unlocked unique heroes.
- **Confirmed:** When selected, the twins receive personal dialogue about the rescue and betrayal. When not selected, they remain present as civil-protection NPCs supporting surveillance, evacuation, or custody of the infiltrator.
- **Confirmed:** Their visual identities will be derived from a user-provided photograph of a teenage girl, with the brother designed as a distinct related character rather than a duplicate.
- **Confirmed:** Final artwork follows the cel-shaded visual language in `C:\AI\Codex\Pictures\FightForPeace\reference`.
- **Confirmed:** Their costumes are modern, practical, fight-style-specific, and protective. Both wear protective boots and appropriate limb and body protection.
- **Confirmed:** Both heroes obey the existing unique-person rule: duplicate selection is not allowed, and every hero remains independently viable in solo play.

### 6.1 Wind Sentinel — 风神

- **Confirmed:** Wind Sentinel is the sister.
- **Confirmed:** Her personality is that of an observant protector: compassionate, quick-thinking, quietly confident, and capable of dry humor. She acts rapidly to protect others but does not attack without cause.
- **Confirmed:** After the infiltrator's betrayal, she briefly questions whether rescuing him was a mistake. Her growth affirms that compassion was correct while careful verification and professional vigilance were missing; she becomes more disciplined without becoming cynical.
- **Confirmed:** Her calm precision contrasts with Fire Warden's more forceful temperament, and her combat speed expresses awareness and fast decision-making rather than recklessness.
- **Confirmed:** She uses a fast karate-derived fighting style.
- **Confirmed:** Her identity emphasizes precise linear strikes, rapid combinations, evasive footwork, sweeps, quick entry, aerial pursuit, repositioning, and whiff punishment.
- **Confirmed:** Her mechanical emphasis is a mobile whiff-punisher and route creator rather than continuous rushdown or passive counter-karate. She makes attacks miss, enters quickly, confirms a short combination, repositions the target, and exits before retaliation.
- **Confirmed:** Successful whiff punishment earns stronger route access than reckless frontal pressure. Side-switches and angled finishers help move enemies away from civilians, hazards, or protected greenhouse equipment.
- **Confirmed:** Her sweeps, launchers, aerial pursuit, cancels, and co-op handoffs obey the shared ground-window, juggle-budget, hit-stun, scaling, and anti-infinite systems. Basic three-action routes remain accessible while character-specific cancels reward accuracy and movement.
- **Confirmed:** Speed and mobility are balanced by lower individual damage, lower guard pressure, and appropriately committed recovery on her strongest routes.
- **Confirmed:** Her Representative Stage 2 normal-action catalogue targets fifteen authored actions distributed as eight standing, three aerial, two ground, one dash, and one low attack. Throws, guards, counters, Combo Breakers, weapon actions, Specials, and Ultimates are counted separately.
- **Confirmed:** The eight-action standing allocation emphasizes grounded karate precision and a unique whiff-punish branch. Three aerial actions provide complete coverage without duplicating River Scout's stronger aerial specialization, while the two ground actions provide an extension and closing finisher rather than a submission-focused kit.
- **Confirmed:** Wind Sentinel uses a grounded karate catalogue with wind-metaphor names and restrained motion effects rather than strict real-world terminology or supernatural wind attacks.

- **Confirmed:** Her working dash action is **Windline Entry**, a fast positional-route starter with no invulnerability. Her working low action is **Grass-Cutter Sweep**, a narrow dash-state sweep that may stagger on a clean hit and trip only on an eligible counter-hit or finisher state.

- **Confirmed:** **Gale Passage** (`Forward + U`) is a fast advancing side kick with a conditional step-through strike. It side-switches only on valid contact and is punishable when blocked or missed.
- **Confirmed:** **Skyward Sentinel** (`Backward + U`) is a brief retreat into a rising roundhouse, providing an anti-air and reversal answer with committed recovery when baited.
- **Confirmed:** **Four-Wind Vigil** (`H`) is her fixed Ultimate: a controlled multi-angle sequence against the primary target that redirects nearby enemies outward, applies strong boss Resolve pressure, and finishes by creating rescue space around a partner or protected objective.

- **Deferred:** Final localized names, meter behavior, frame data, invulnerability or evasion windows, trajectories, reactions, cancel links, targeting, crowd radius, Resolve damage, animation, and effects remain Combat Lab and production values.

### 6.2 Fire Warden — 火神

- **Confirmed:** Fire Warden is the brother.
- **Confirmed:** His personality uses **Disciplined Intensity**: warm and loyal toward the village, direct in speech, and forceful once a threat is verified, while trained to control rather than obey his anger.
- **Confirmed:** He is protective of Wind Sentinel without treating her as fragile. He trusts her judgment and recognizes that her speed and observation often create his strongest openings.
- **Confirmed:** The infiltrator's betrayal angers him, but his character growth channels that anger into firm accountability rather than revenge. He supports capture and lawful judgment instead of execution.
- **Confirmed:** His straightforward earnestness contrasts with Wind Sentinel's quiet confidence and dry humor. His fire identity represents momentum, courage, and controlled force rather than supernatural flame or uncontrolled rage.
- **Confirmed:** His fighting system combines Sanda or Sanshou, streamlined modern kickboxing, and military-oriented Qinna joint-control principles.
- **Confirmed:** His identity emphasizes efficient entries, close-range pressure, kick catches, clinch control, trips, takedowns, joint-control transitions, and explosive finishers.
- **Confirmed:** His mechanical emphasis is a **Momentum Converter** rather than a slow heavy grappler or pure kickboxing rushdown character. Direct strikes establish pressure and close distance; confirmed hits, Counters, or specifically eligible attacks open brief Sanda catches, clinches, trips, or Qinna conclusions.
- **Confirmed:** Kickboxing supplies entry pressure, Sanda converts earned openings, and Qinna controls the route's conclusion. Qinna actions remain short and battlefield-safe rather than prolonged stationary submissions.
- **Confirmed:** Catch and clinch transitions require eligible contacts, authored timing, and valid target state; they never automatically catch attacks. Large enemies and bosses permit stronger control only during authored vulnerable or Resolve-broken states.
- **Confirmed:** Trips and takedowns use explicit `opensGroundWindow` or `terminalKnockdown` outcomes, and cooperative conversion never refreshes the target's scaling, stun, juggle, ground, or environmental budgets.
- **Confirmed:** Strong damage and control are balanced by shorter effective range and more committed recovery.
- **Confirmed:** His Representative Stage 2 normal-action catalogue targets fifteen authored actions distributed as seven standing, three aerial, three ground, one dash, and one low attack. Throws, grab extensions, kick catches, Counters, Combo Breakers, weapon actions, Specials, and Ultimates are counted separately.
- **Confirmed:** The seven standing actions provide the shared three-action fallback plus advancing, retreating, and two stage-depth options. Three aerial actions preserve complete air coverage without making him an aerial specialist. Three ground actions provide one extension and two mutually exclusive conclusions: a controlled cooperative release and a heavier terminal takedown.
- **Confirmed:** Fire Warden's representative catalogue uses physical Sanda, streamlined kickboxing, and Qinna-inspired control. Forge and fire terminology is metaphorical; restrained heat-coloured impact accents may communicate force, but he does not wield literal fire powers.
- **Confirmed:** His seven working standing actions are **Spark Jab**, a fast neutral lead-hand starter; **Hammer Cross**, a committed reverse punch with stronger hit confirmation; **Furnace Heel**, the separating neutral-chain roundhouse finisher; **Flashpoint Drive**, an advancing knee-and-elbow entry that may cancel into an eligible grab only after valid contact; **Cinder Check**, a short retreating push kick; **Rising Brand**, a depth-up rising knee with controlled launch potential; and **Forge-Side Hook**, a depth-down shifting hook that turns the target toward close range.

His working low action is **Ashline Reap**, a short Sanda foot sweep with stronger trip pressure but less reach and speed than Wind Sentinel's Grass-Cutter.

A confirmed hit against an eligible enemy may convert into a brief Sanda clinch and directional takedown; a miss, block, or ineligible target produces punishable recovery and never becomes an automatic grab through guard.

## 17. Stage evolution policy and feature comparison

### 17.1 Stage preservation policy

- **Confirmed:** Stage 1 remains the stable foundation and is not retrofitted merely because a later stage introduces a richer feature. Players should experience the campaign's development through additions and changes in new stages.
- **Confirmed:** Previous-stage content is reopened only for a critical defect, progression blocker, safety, legal, accessibility, deterministic-synchronization, engine-compatibility, or confirmed narrative or mechanical conflict. Preference alone or the existence of a newer feature is not sufficient reason.
- **Confirmed:** Cross-stage engine corrections preserve the earlier stage's intended content, difficulty identity, records, and versioned replay meaning wherever technically possible.
- **Confirmed:** Stage 1 therefore retains the Black-Iron Enforcer as its Hard-and-higher named challenge enemy. Extreme and Ultimate increase formations, group attacks, variants, and mastery without adding a newly authored difficulty-exclusive Stage 1 enemy.
- **Confirmed:** Stage 2 is the first stage to introduce a named Extreme-only challenge role, the Jackal Signalman. Ultimate develops the complete Stage 2 roster through master variants and rare formations rather than requiring another new enemy art package.
- **Confirmed:** Campaign-wide systems introduced later may index compatible material that Stage 1 already owns—for example, placing unlocked Stage 1 pictures, movies, and music in the Museum—but do not require new Stage 1 encounters, artifacts, weapons, enemies, or route branches unless a critical issue demands them.
